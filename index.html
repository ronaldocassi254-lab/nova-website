What makes Viva Nova different?

Most sports drinks rely on sugar. Viva Nova uses a five-mineral electrolyte blend, 10% coconut water and B-vitamins, with just 3g of sugar and 25 calories per 500ml can.

Do you sell bottles as well as cans?

Yes. Every flavour comes in a 500ml can or a 500ml bottle with a resealable sports cap. The drink inside is exactly the same. Pick the format on any product, or switch the whole shop to bottles.

Can I buy just one?

Yes. Cans come as a single, a 4-pack, a 12-pack or a 24-pack. Bottles come as a single, a 6-pack, a 12-pack or a 24-pack. Bigger packs cost less each.

When should I drink it?

Before, during or after training. One can about 30 minutes before, small sips every 15–20 minutes during, and one after to rehydrate. Each sport page has a detailed game plan.

Does it contain caffeine? Is it vegan?

Viva Nova is completely caffeine-free and vegan-friendly, so you can drink it any time of day.

How does the subscription work?

Subscriptions are available on 12 and 24-packs. You'll get 15% off and a delivery every 4 weeks, and you can skip, swap flavours or cancel whenever you like.

How much is delivery?

UK delivery is free on orders over £30. Otherwise it's £3.95. Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are dispatched the same day.
